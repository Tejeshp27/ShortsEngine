# ShortsEngine Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ShortsEngine is a personal tool used only by its owner, Tejesh Patel, to manage his own social media accounts.

Data collected: when you connect a TikTok account, ShortsEngine receives your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), account statistics (followers, likes, video count), and a list of your public videos and their view counts.

How it is used: this data is only used to show channel performance on the private ShortsEngine dashboard and to upload videos as drafts that you review before posting.

Storage: access tokens are encrypted and stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deletion: you can disconnect your account at any time from the dashboard, which deletes the stored token, or revoke access in your TikTok app settings.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Tejeshp27/ShortsEngine/issues
