ShortsEngine Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What ShortsEngine is

ShortsEngine is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer or server. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account it connects to is the owner's.

Information we access

When you click "Connect with Google", ShortsEngine asks for permission to manage your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API. It uses that permission only to upload videos you have approved in the dashboard and to read basic details about your channel and those uploads.

How information is stored

The OAuth tokens Google returns are encrypted and stored in a local database on the machine running ShortsEngine. They are never sent to anyone else.

Sharing

ShortsEngine does not sell, share, or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access

You can disconnect the account in the ShortsEngine dashboard, or revoke access at any time at Google Account permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless.

YouTube and Google

ShortsEngine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
